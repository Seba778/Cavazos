Cabrito from Monterrey

100% cabrito regio

Imported from Monterrey, N.L.

Our kid goat comes from Monterrey, Nuevo León, and is roasted 100% over mesquite wood and charcoal, slowly, on an open fire, the way it is done in northern Mexico.

Served sizzling

It arrives on a sizzling cast-iron skillet over a bed of onions, with refried beans topped with slow-cooked pork stew, a plate of lettuce, tomato and onion, and your choice of tortillas.
